## Game inspiration

Dayline Dispatch is an independent transit-planning game. Mini Metro by Dinosaur Polo Club helped popularize the genre. Dayline Dispatch uses its own maps, visuals, code, branding, sounds and rules. Mini Metro and Dinosaur Polo Club are credited here as a creative reference.

[Dinosaur Polo Club’s Mini Metro](https://dinopoloclub.com/games/mini-metro/)
